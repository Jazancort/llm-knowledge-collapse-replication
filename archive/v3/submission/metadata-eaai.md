# EAAI Submission — Title & Abstract

## Title

Effective Training Pressure Gates Recursive Knowledge Degradation in LLMs: A Multi-Axis Dose-Response Study

## Abstract

Recursive fine-tuning on synthetic data enables scalable model adaptation. However, recursively retraining models on their own outputs can progressively degrade factual knowledge. The conditions under which recursive degradation emerges, remains bounded, or can be reversed under parameter-efficient fine-tuning remain poorly understood. Through systematic dose-response experiments spanning three backbones (Qwen 2.5 1.5B, Gemma 3 1B, and Gemma 4 E2B), ten recursive generations, and three to five independent seeds per condition, we investigate how recursive factual retention changes as adapter capacity, perturbation magnitude, and synthetic exposure vary. These experiments reveal a common empirical organizing principle, which we term effective training pressure. We find: (1) a sharp regime transition from homeostatic retention (above 90% over ten generations) to progressive degradation as adapter rank increases, with the threshold differing by an order of magnitude across architectures (effective rank 3–6 on Gemma versus 50–88 on Qwen); (2) full fine-tuning learning-rate sweeps on both backbones that reproduce the same qualitative pattern, confirming that the transition is not unique to low-rank adaptation; (3) a rank × learning-rate interaction demonstrating that update capacity and perturbation magnitude jointly determine the operating regime rather than acting as independent axes; (4) a retention/distribution dissociation at intermediate pressure, where output efficiency degrades threefold while factual retention remains near 90%; and (5) restoration of near-homeostatic behavior through ~5% reduction in synthetic exposure at the Qwen boundary configuration. These findings characterize recursive degradation as a pressure-dependent phenomenon whose stability depends on a multidimensional control landscape, providing an empirical basis for configuring recursive training pipelines.
