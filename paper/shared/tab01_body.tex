% EDIT ONLY THIS FILE.  Symbols listed in order of first appearance.
% Adapter-level quantities (erank, ||.||_F) are averaged across adapted
% LoRA modules within a model.
$K_0$
  & Set of items in $Q$ answered correctly at generation~0;
    $|K_0|$ denotes its cardinality;
    values: $|K_0|=78$ (main Qwen runs),
            $|K_0|=46$ (Gemma~3 rank sweep),
            $|K_0|=44$ (G2 dose),
            $|K_{0,\mathrm{G4}}|=76$ (Gemma~4 E2B) \\
$R(t)$
  & Factual retention at generation~$t$: fraction of $K_0$ items
    still correct \\
$\theta^{(0)}$
  & Pretrained backbone weights \\
$\theta^{(t)}$
  & Adapter-augmented weights at generation~$t$ \\
$\Delta W = BA$
  & Low-rank LoRA update matrix
    ($B \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times r}$, $A \in \mathbb{R}^{r\times d}$) \\
$\sigma_i$
  & $i$-th singular value of $\Delta W$ \\
$p_i$
  & Normalized singular weight,
    $p_i = \sigma_i / \sum_j \sigma_j$;
    $p_i \ge 0$ and $\sum_i p_i = 1$ \\
$\mathrm{erank}(\Delta W)$
  & Effective rank:
    $\exp\!\bigl(-\sum_i p_i \log p_i\bigr)$
    (Eq.~\ref{eq:erank}) \\
$\|\Delta W\|_F$
  & Frobenius norm of composed update, averaged across modules \\
$d(t)$
  & Parameter drift: mean absolute weight change
    $\theta^{(t)}-\theta^{(0)}$ \\
$\eta$
  & Learning rate \\
$\Pi$
  & Operational pressure index:
    $\mathrm{erank}(\Delta W)\cdot(\eta/10^{-5})$ \\
$f$
  & Exposure dose: fraction of training items removed \\
$r_H$, $r_D$
  & Empirically observed rank transition intervals (Qwen-specific):
    $r_H \in (64,128)$, $r_D \in (128,256)$;
    not universal thresholds \\
$T$
  & Evaluation horizon (10 generations in main experiments) \\
$\mathrm{ContentEff}(t)$
  & Retention divided by mean synthetic response length \\
$\mathrm{Persist}(t)$
  & Fraction of $K_0$ items whose response at generation~$t$
    matches generation~0 \\
$\mathrm{SDI\text{-}3}$
  & Synthetic Drift Index: composite of verbosity, diversity,
    and instability changes \\
